Who we are

Kalyd is a transport management system developed by Masev S.R.L., Str. Zorilor nr. 6, bl. Z7, ap. 28, 700377 Iași, Romania, fiscal code (CUI) 49017137, VAT ID RO49017137. Masev S.R.L. is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Questions about your data: info@masev.ro or admin@mail.kalyd.app.

We only collect what you send us through the site, plus the technical data any website receives. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who else handles your data

These providers process data only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement.

Cookies and analytics

The site sets no cookies. It saves two settings in your browser, your light or dark theme and your language, and never sends them to us.

We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It stores nothing on your device. Visits are grouped by a code that changes every day, so we see totals such as pages, countries and devices, never individual visitors. Our basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used (art. 6(1)(f)).

Transfers outside the EU

Some providers are based in the USA. Where data leaves the EU, it is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, to limit how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. You can also object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Email info@masev.ro or admin@mail.kalyd.app and we answer within one month.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Romanian data protection authority, Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP), B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru 28-30, Sector 1, 010336 Bucharest, dataprotection.ro, or to the authority in your own EU country. In Belgium this is the Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), dataprotectionauthority.be.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when the way we handle data changes, for example when Kalyd becomes its own company. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects data you already gave us, we tell you by email.
