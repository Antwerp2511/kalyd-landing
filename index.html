How it works

From inbox to invoice, in four steps.

Step 1

An order comes in.

By email, exactly how your customers already send it today. Body text, PDF or scan.

Image · Step 1 · Order email arrives
Step 2

Kalyd builds the order.

Parties, loading reference, addresses, dates, pallets and price land on the dispatch board automatically. Dangerous goods are flagged as ADR the moment the order is read.

Image · Step 2 · Order on the dispatch board, ADR flag
Step 3

You assign a driver.

One click on the dispatch board. WhatsApp opens with the job pre-filled.

Image · Step 3 · WhatsApp assignment to driver
Step 4

It gets invoiced.

Delivery confirmed, invoice generated from the same order record and sent via Peppol.

Image · Step 4 · Delivered, invoice sent via Peppol
The AI inside

It reads orders the way your best dispatcher does.

No templates to set up and no new inbox. Kalyd works on the emails you already get.

  • It reads any order. Email body, PDF attachment or scan, in Dutch, French or English.
  • It builds the order. Parties, loading reference, addresses, dates, pallets and price, filled in for you.
  • It spots dangerous goods. ADR loads are flagged automatically, so the right driver and truck get assigned.
  • You stay in control. Unclear fields are highlighted for a quick check before anything reaches a driver.
